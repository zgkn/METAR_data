Barb colour shows visibility: amber under 5 km, orange under 3 km, red under 1.5 km (thicker as it gets worse). Each barb points toward where the wind blows from; half barb 5 kt, full barb 10 kt, pennant 50 kt. Open circle: calm (under 1 kt) or variable direction. Dashed ring: no report within 30 min of the map time. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. When zoomed out, overlapping stations are thinned; zoom in to see them all.
